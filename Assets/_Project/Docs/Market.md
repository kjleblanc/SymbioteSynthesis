# Market Snapshot & Risks

## Audience Segments
- Strategy/roguelite PC players (Steam wishlisters).
- Idle/merge curious who want depth (Steam Discovery Queue).
- Creators: build/guide meta (YouTube/Twitch).

## Session Length Target
- 20–30 min sessions; 45–90 min full run.

## Price Hypothesis
- $9.99 base; launch −10% week one. DLC-free at launch.

## Key Risks & Mitigations
- Fusion discoverability → Discovery tab + recipe hints + pity on new outputs.
- Colorblind reliance → Icon shapes/patterns, colorblind palettes.
- Scope creep → MoSCoW MVP; explicit won’t-haves.
- Readability in combat → VFX throttle; damage numbers caps; profile GC.
- Content runway → Recipe generator pipeline; test harness for balance.

## Competitor Notes
Many merges rely on store currency; **we lean pure spatial economy**—strong differentiator for store page.
