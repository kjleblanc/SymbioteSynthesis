# High Concept (1-pager)

## Hook
Fuse living components on a hex board to grow bio-punk symbiotes, then watch them auto-battle waves on the same grid. **Three merges per turn; no currency—just space and discovery.**

## Pillars
- **Deterministic Discovery:** Seeded fusion compendium per run; persistent Collection across runs.
- **Board Is Economy:** Sticky hex pieces, no gravity; space tension = strategy.
- **One Board, Two Phases:** Build and fight in place; positioning matters.
- **Readable Complexity:** “2+2=3” fusion grammar, traits combine from components.

## Audience
Merge/auto-battler fans who like Slay-the-Spire-style runs; PC indie strategy players; light theorycrafters.

## Comps & Differentiation
- **Comps:** Auto Chess (auto-battle), Merge & Blade (merge+battle), Slay the Spire (map/run meta).
- **Differentiation:** Hex, sticky no-gravity merges; deterministic, logged fusion system; economy = board space only.

## Why Now
Merge mechanics are mainstream; auto-battlers still underserved on PC with tight scope indie takes.
